## Design Consistency Tips

### Colors:
- Primary: #3480f1 (Piano Triads blue)
- Secondary: White/light gray backgrounds
- Accents: Purple gradients (matching app)

### Typography:
- **Headlines**: Bold, 48-72px
- **Subheads**: Medium weight, 24-36px  
- **Body**: Regular, 16-20px

### Piano Keyboard:
- White keys: #FFFFFF with subtle shadow
- Black keys: #000000 
- Highlighted keys: #3480f1 (brand blue)

### File Export:
- **Size**: 1200x630 pixels
- **Format**: JPG, 90% quality
- **File size**: Keep under 500KB each
